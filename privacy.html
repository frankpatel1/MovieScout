Privacy Policy

Last Revised: September 2026 • MovieScout

1. Overview & Information Source

MovieScout is an open entertainment discovery platform. Film and television metadata, posters, and trailers are powered by The Movie Database (TMDB) API. MovieScout is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

2. Data Collection & Cookies

MovieScout does not require user accounts or collect private credentials. We store lightweight preferences (such as selected media filter, sorting preferences, and cookie settings) locally inside your browser's localStorage.

3. Third-Party Service Links

Our service provides embedded YouTube movie trailers and links to TMDB. When engaging with embedded players, you may be subject to Google and YouTube's privacy policies.

4. Your Rights & Contacts

Because we do not maintain centralized user identity databases, no personal profiles are kept on remote servers. For questions regarding our data practices, contact privacy@moviescout.example.com.
